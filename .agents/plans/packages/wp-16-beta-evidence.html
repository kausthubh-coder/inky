WP-16 · private-beta product evidence

See what a scan or assignment actually cost, and who hit the error.

The private beta already consented to product evidence. PostHog should receive the facts needed to change the product: model, reasoning, duration, tokens, cost, school context, and real error text. Secrets stay out. School passwords, cookies, answers, and the school page stay local.

Observable outcome

Student scans or works→typed event + person properties→secret strip only→PostHog

A finished scan or assignment run is one envelope with model, reasoning effort, duration, token counts, estimated cost when known, student name, school URL, and course or assignment titles. Errors keep their message after secret stripping. Clerk name and email attach to the person profile.

Small ownership model

  • Reuse TelemetryService. Expand the closed event map. Do not add a second analytics stack.
  • PiAgentRuntime accumulates last-run usage from Pi session messages and exposes it to the existing scan and assignment capture helpers in main.ts.
  • Person properties live on identify / $set. Event properties stay allowlisted.
  • The school WebContentsView still never loads PostHog.

Allowed vocabulary

  • Existing product flow events, plus studi_assignment_finished and studi_model_selected.
  • Model id, reasoning effort, duration, input/output/cache tokens, tool-call count, estimated USD cost, student name, school URL, course titles, assignment title, Clerk email/name, error message.
  • Never passwords, cookies, tokens, authorization headers, page HTML, assignment answers, prompts, or school-browser replay.

Explicit exclusions

  • No dashboards, feature flags, warehouse, or generic OpenTelemetry stack.
  • No raw transcript upload and no PostHog in the school view.
  • No second queue or schema-generation framework.
  • Do not restore a sign-in telemetry opt-in. Settings still owns the switch.

Pass conditions

  • Scan and assignment completion events include model, reasoning, and duration; token/cost fields appear when Pi reports usage.
  • Personal fields are stored. Secret canaries are still stripped. Arbitrary undeclared properties are still rejected.
  • School browser isolation and opt-out/sign-out identity reset remain.
  • Existing auth, contracts, storage, agent, Electron, and packaging gates stay green.
