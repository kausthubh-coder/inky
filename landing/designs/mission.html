Mission

Homework help you can see. A last click that stays yours.

Studi exists so a student can finish the work on the real school page, watch every step, and still be the one who submits.

What we are building

A Windows app that finds what’s due, attempts one assignment at a time, and stops. Not a hidden agent. Not a website you leave open in Chrome. Not a quiz machine.

What we will not do

  • × Start or click through quizzes, tests, or exams
  • × Ask for a school password or store one
  • × Upload your files for you
  • × Claim we are undetectable. There is no stealth mode

School stays on the computer

Assignments, drafts, and the school page live on the machine. The cloud gets an account and a waitlist email. Not the essay. Not the cookies.

Your syllabus still wins

If a class bans this, don’t run it there. Studi does the homework in a page you can watch. The choice to use it is yours.
