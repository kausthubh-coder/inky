Make Inky’s school check visible

Implementation plan · September 10, 2026

A persistent card above the real week board shows what Inky is doing and one clear next action. Preserve the existing calendar, assignments, fonts, and mascot.

  • Ready / complete: Scan for homework starts a check directly.
  • Scanning: blue card, scanning Inky, current step and View scan.
  • Needs you: warm yellow card, explicit paused status, handoff reason and Help Inky. Open the school browser, finish the request, then continue.
  • Failed / partial: incomplete status and retry. Saved assignments remain visible.
  • Browser busy: name the assignment, let the student wait, or explicitly stop eligible work and scan. Await worker abort, cancel, verify lease release, then request a scan. A failed stop never starts the scan. No automatic scan queue.
  • Browser icon toggles open/closed with accessible names and expanded state.
  • Separate App updates from Notifications; show unread count and meaningful icons for handoff, scan, review, and failure.
  • Rename homework feedback so it says what it reports. Keep class coverage secondary and retain unresolved identity records.
