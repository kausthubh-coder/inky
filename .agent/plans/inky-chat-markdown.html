What the student sees

You

Your bubble stays exactly as you typed it. Homework titles with stars, formulas, and emails do not turn into formatting.

Inky

Emphasis, lists, a scrap of code, a real link. Headings stay at speaking size. No docs-site stack, no syntax-highlighter chrome, no remote pictures.

One renderer

Home chat, assignment sidebar, desk drawer, and school-check talk all use the same Inky markdown. While a scan is running, Inky’s replies show as bubbles under the report — not only later in Scan details. Your bubbles stay as typed. Stored text does not change.

  • Only http, https, and mailto links are clickable.
  • In the desktop window, those links open in the system browser instead of navigating Studi away.
  • A message with no markdown still looks like a spoken paragraph.
